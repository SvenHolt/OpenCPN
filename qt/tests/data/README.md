Test data

* `1B5X02NE.000` – S-57 sample cell from the GDAL autotest suite
  (https://github.com/OSGeo/gdal, autotest/ogr/data/s57, MIT license).
* `rgbsmall.kap` – BSB sample from the GDAL autotest suite
  (autotest/gdrivers/data/bsb, MIT license).
* `quikscat.grb` – GRIB1 sample from the GDAL autotest suite
  (autotest/gdrivers/data/grib, MIT license).
* `gfs_simple.grb2`, `gfs_complex.grb2` – NOAA GFS 1° forecast subsets
  (NOMADS, public domain); simple packing and complex packing with spatial
  differencing.
